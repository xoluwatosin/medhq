# Contract templates, an annex library, and issuing to many at once

Today a contract is authored one person at a time and every annex is a file you attach by hand. For a role you are hiring five people into, that is the same document written five times.

This adds three things, in plain terms:

**A contract template.** You write the role's contract once: the wording, the annexes it carries, and the terms that are the same for everyone. It sits in a template list you can reuse, copy and retire.

**An annex library.** Each annex, job description, code of conduct, confidentiality undertaking and the rest, becomes a document in the system that you click and edit like a clause. Some annexes stay as an uploaded file where that suits better, and each annex says which of the two it is. Your HTML drafts load in as the starting wording.

**Issue to many.** From the template you pick the candidates who accepted the role, see them in one table with their terms already filled in and anything missing flagged, open any single one to read the whole document if you want to, then issue to all of them in one action.

What does not change: the letter itself, the signing page, the signature evidence, the PDF and the copy filed on the person's record. A template produces the same contract the editor produces today.
