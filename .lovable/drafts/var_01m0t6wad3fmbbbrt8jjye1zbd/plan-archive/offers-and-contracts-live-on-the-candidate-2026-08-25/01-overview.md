# Offers and contracts live on the candidate

Today the work is split. Offers are made from the candidate's own Work tab, but contracts are built from a separate Workforce screen where you pick the person again and retype terms that the system already knows. That split is the problem.

This plan collapses both into one tab on the candidate profile: **Offers and contracts**, sitting alongside Verification, Profile, Matching, Applications and Activity. You open a person, you go to that tab, and everything about hiring them happens there with their own data already filled in.

What changes for you:

- No top-level place to create an offer or a contract from. You always start from the person.
- The offer form opens with their name, profession, licence, location, availability window and stated rate already in it. You adjust rather than type.
- A contract is built from the offer they accepted, so the terms carry over instead of being re-entered.
- The pack you send, and every event on it, stays on that tab as one history: offered, opened, accepted, contract drafted, issued, signed, countersigned, filed.
