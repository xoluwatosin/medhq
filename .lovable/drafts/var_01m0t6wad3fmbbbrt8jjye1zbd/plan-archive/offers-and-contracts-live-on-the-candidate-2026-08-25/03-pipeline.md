## What the tab holds

One tab, read top to bottom as a single story about hiring this person.

**1. The header line.** Who they are in hiring terms: profession, licence state, location, whether their documents are accepted, and whether they are already staff. If something blocks a contract, it says so here in a sentence rather than leaving you to discover it at the end.

**2. Offers.** Every offer made to this person, newest first, with its state and what they said. "Offer work" opens the composer already carrying:

- name, profession and licence from their profile
- location and travel radius from their preferences
- the next dates they are actually free, from their availability
- their stated rate expectation, as the starting figure
- the opportunity they were shortlisted for, when they came from one

You change what needs changing and send. The offer appears in their portal exactly as it does now.

**3. Contracts.** Every contract for this person, with its state and the signing link. "Build contract" starts from the accepted offer, so job title, start date, pay, pattern, location and notice are already carried over, and only the wording and the annexes remain to review before issuing.

**4. The pack history.** One combined trail across both: offered, opened, accepted or declined, contract drafted, issued, viewed, signed, countersigned, filed to their documents. Timestamps and who did it.

## What moves and what stays

- The offers half already lives on the candidate's Work tab; it moves into the new tab with the pre-fill added. Leave and engagements stay where they are, on Work.
- The contract builder, signing flow and PDF are unchanged as machinery. What changes is where you start them from and what is filled in for you.
- The Workforce screen stays for what it is genuinely for: the roll of staff and their compliance. It stops being a place you author a contract from and instead links through to the person's tab.
- Nothing about the signing page, the candidate portal, or the stored contract document changes.
