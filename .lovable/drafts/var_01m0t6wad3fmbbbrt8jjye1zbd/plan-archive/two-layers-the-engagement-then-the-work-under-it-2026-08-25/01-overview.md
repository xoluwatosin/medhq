# Two layers: the engagement, then the work under it

You have named the real split, and it is not offer versus contract. It is:

- **The engagement** — the relationship, established once and papered once. Full time employment, a fixed-term placement, or a bank/locum agreement covering a scope of work for a period. This is the thing that gets signed.
- **The work** — the actual shifts and assignments offered under that engagement, each with its own dates, hours and rate. A full-time medic's shifts sit under their employment contract. A bank nurse's shifts sit under their bank agreement. Neither needs new paper.

So offers and contracts stop being two halves of one hire. They become two different objects with different lifetimes: one engagement, many work offers.

```text
engagement (signed once, covers a period and a scope)
   |
   +-- work offer: 12 Sep, night, NGN 45,000
   +-- work offer: 14-18 Sep, days, NGN 200,000
   +-- work offer: ongoing rota from 1 Oct
```

The unification you asked for happens **inside the engagement**: proposing terms and papering them is one continuous record, not an offer you then rebuild as a contract. The variation happens by engagement type.
