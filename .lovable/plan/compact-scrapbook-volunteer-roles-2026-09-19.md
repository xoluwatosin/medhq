# Compact scrapbook volunteer roles

## Goal

Replace the three oversized volunteer boxes with the selected layered scrapbook stack. Keep every role, description and application destination unchanged while making the complete set substantially shorter and more lively on mobile.

## Design

- Use three compact white paper slips with thin Late borders, small offset shadows and subtle alternating angles.
- Remove the full Pale Sky treatment from role 02. Pale Sky becomes a restrained tape, edge or stamped detail only.
- Retain the 01, 02 and 03 sequence at a smaller scale within each slip.
- Give each role one distinct Heard motif:
  - **Peer Listener:** a centred Pale Sky tape label and the existing training note, reduced to a compact pinned-note treatment.
  - **Social Media Volunteer:** a corner pin or folded-paper detail, without an unsupported label such as “Most popular”.
  - **Counsellor, psychologist or clinician:** a restrained Heard postmark or dotted stamp detail.
- Keep Figtree, the existing Heard palette and square editorial character. Do not import the prototype’s substitute fonts, colours or invented wording.
- Use restrained staggered entrance movement and a small paper-settle interaction on pointer devices; honour reduced-motion settings.

## Mobile layout

- Target approximately 220–300px per role rather than the current 480–570px boxes.
- Reduce internal spacing, numeral size and button height while preserving readable type and 44px minimum touch targets.
- Keep all role information visible without requiring an accordion or extra tap.
- Prevent long labels and the professional role title from clipping or forcing excessive card height.

## Desktop layout

- Keep the three-role row, but replace equal tall boxes with a lighter staggered composition.
- Align the actions visually without forcing every paper slip to the current 540px height.
- Preserve clear scanning and avoid overlaps between neighbouring slips.

## Verification

- Check the section at 390×700, 430×786 and 430×900, plus laptop and desktop widths.
- Confirm all three application links retain the correct role parameter.
- Confirm no clipping, horizontal overflow or unreadable overlap, including reduced-motion mode.
- Confirm the page remains within the established Heard element system and the preview build is healthy.
