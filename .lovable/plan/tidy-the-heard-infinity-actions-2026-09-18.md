# Tidy the Heard infinity actions

## Goal

Keep the existing Heard homepage design unchanged in character, while making the two connector lines and action pills feel precisely placed around the official infinity mark.

## Changes

- Keep **Be heard** on the left and **Read a letter** on the right, with their current wording, colours and destinations.
- Retain the official infinity mark, current hero copy, palette and overall composition.
- Adjust each connector so it meets the outside curve of the mark cleanly from its opposite side.
- Refine the line curves and endpoints so they do not appear detached, collide with the mark or crowd the pills.
- Give both action pills consistent breathing room and balanced optical spacing without shrinking their touch areas.
- Tune the same composition separately for mobile and desktop so the joins remain clean at both sizes.

## Verification

- Check the complete opening screen on narrow and tall phones, short laptops and standard desktop sizes.
- Confirm both action pills remain clickable and lead to the existing pages.
- Confirm no overlap, clipping, horizontal overflow or unintended movement of the headline and supporting line.
- Confirm the build and browser console remain clear.

## Boundaries

No redesign, new element, wording, colour, logo, route, motion, backend or non-Heard change.
