# Quality assurance and repair of the 74 new SEO pages

## Goal
Make every new page fulfil the reason someone searched for and opened it. Preserve the existing Clinical Home Care visual design, governed prices, approved claims, routes and page count.

## Audit finding
The 74 pages currently inherit a generic introductory sentence and generic checklist/card patterns. This gives them the correct topic and layout, but often does not answer the title. The problem is most obvious on question and comparison pages, but it also affects service, childcare, staffing and jobs pages.

## Changes

### 1. Add a direct answer to every page
- Replace the generated one-line introduction with an explicit, page-specific answer stored with that page.
- Put the answer immediately below the opening section, before audiences, prices and process details.
- Use a clear answer heading suited to the page, such as “The short answer”, “What the service provides”, “Staffing options” or “Routes into work”.
- Keep answers concise, usually one short paragraph plus a small decision list where the query needs comparison or selection.

### 2. Repair each page family
- **Question and guide pages:** answer the exact question, then explain how to choose. For “Who do I need after surgery?”, distinguish a nurse, caregiver and physiotherapist by the actual need, and explain when a combined plan may be appropriate.
- **Care pages:** state what the service is, what professional delivers it, what it can include and the important safety or hospital boundary.
- **Childcare pages:** define the role or support, distinguish it from nearby roles, and state what is agreed with the family or school.
- **Staffing pages:** state which professionals or teams Medic Connect can supply and whether the route is locum, temporary, contract, managed workforce or permanent placement.
- **Jobs pages:** state the real routes into work: advertised opportunities, shifts or assignments, contracts, employment and permanent placement. Registration remains only entry to the candidate pool, not a job route or offer.

### 3. Replace generic supporting content
- Remove generic “What to consider” lines that merely repeat the title.
- Give guide pages route-specific checklists, comparisons or decision points.
- Make service cards describe the actual service rather than repeating “assessment / agreed support / records” on every page.
- Keep “How this works” as concise expandable process information, but do not use it as a substitute for the page’s answer.

### 4. Apply governance and safety checks
- Use only approved modules, governed public prices and existing confirmed service statements.
- Do not invent clinical advice, outcomes, availability, coverage, qualifications, prices or guarantees.
- Keep Lagos, Abuja/FCT, Ogun State and Ibadan/Oyo as served markets; do not present Port Harcourt/Rivers as served.
- Keep emergency, prescribing, hospital-care and third-party transport boundaries where relevant.

### 5. Add automated content-quality gates
- Require all 74 records to have a non-empty, route-specific direct answer.
- Reject duplicate direct answers and generic placeholder patterns.
- Require question pages to contain an actual answer, comparison or decision rule.
- Require jobs pages to describe a genuine work route and retain the no-job-offer qualification.
- Validate fee references, module references, unique routes, titles and metadata.

### 6. Acceptance review
- Check every one of the 74 routes renders successfully and exposes its direct answer in the page text.
- Review representative pages from all five families on phone and desktop, including the reported post-surgery page.
- Run the SEO content tests, full test suite and build diagnostics.
- Report any page whose answer cannot be completed from governed facts rather than filling the gap with invented copy.

## Out of scope
- No new pages or routes.
- No public pricing changes.
- No redesign of the page layout.
- No changes to Care, Talent, Workforce, Finance or other operational systems.
- No publication or indexing-state changes.
