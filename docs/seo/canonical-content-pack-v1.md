## Publication rules carried into the registry

- A care request is acknowledged within 24 hours. This is not a promise that care starts within 24 hours.
- Care start times are not guaranteed.
- A formal assessment is required before care; it may be proportionate and immediately pre-intervention.
- The assessment fee is a fixed ₦35,000, paid before assessment, refunded if Medic Connect cannot provide the assessment.
- Police/FCID checks are not universal. In Lagos they may be arranged on request.
- Licence information is collected and reviewed; regulator-side verification is not claimed.
- Medic Connect is not an emergency service; it triages, escalates and coordinates third-party transport.
- No portal or family reporting promise.
- No nationwide availability claim.
- Third-party costs are disclosed before proceeding.

## Deliberately not claimable

- Care commencement within 24 hours, same-day availability, instant care, universal on-call clinical support.
- Guaranteed nationwide availability.
- Port Harcourt/Rivers as an active SEO market until confirmed.
- Universal police/FCID checking.
- Universal regulator-side licence verification.
- Universal basic life support training.
- Client or family portal reporting, live dashboards, routine digital family updates.
- Medic Connect-owned ambulance or medevac provision.
- Regulated after-death verification, mortuary or funeral functions performed by Medic Connect.
- Home birth, home-birth preparation, or hospital-only maternity diagnostics.
- Any service price not linked to an approved `service_fees` record, other than the fixed ₦35,000 assessment fee.
- Any named partner relationship without evidence suitable for public use.
- Any clinical-trial sponsor, ethics authority or protocol-owner implication.
