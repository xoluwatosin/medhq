## A. The 33 governed claims

Format: ID — name (risk) — proposed wording — boundary — pages using it.

**CLM-001 HEFAMAA licensing (high) — 5 pages**
"Medic Connect is licensed and accredited by the Health Facility Monitoring and Accreditation
Agency (HEFAMAA), the Lagos State regulator for health facilities."
Boundary: HEFAMAA is a Lagos State regulator; this is not a national licence.

**CLM-002 Professional indemnity insurance (high) — 5 pages**
"Medic Connect maintains current professional indemnity insurance covering the services it
delivers."
Boundary: indemnity only; no other class of cover is claimed.

**CLM-003 Healthcare Federation of Nigeria membership (medium) — 4 pages**
"Medic Connect is a member of the Healthcare Federation of Nigeria."
Boundary: membership, not accreditation or endorsement.

**CLM-004 LAWMA medical waste (medium) — 0 pages**
"Clinical waste generated during care in Lagos is handled through medical waste arrangements
with the Lagos Waste Management Authority (LAWMA)."
Boundary: Lagos arrangement only.

**CLM-005 Assessment fee (medium) — 18 pages**
"The Medic Connect formal care assessment is charged at a fixed fee of 35,000 naira, payable
before the assessment takes place."
Boundary: fixed, never "from"; paid in advance. Rendered from fee record PUB-ASSESSMENT.

**CLM-006 Assessment refund (medium) — 18 pages**
"If Medic Connect cannot provide the assessment, the assessment fee is refunded."
Boundary: the only refund circumstance stated.

**CLM-007 Response within 24 hours (medium) — 18 pages**
"Medic Connect responds to a care request within 24 hours of receiving it. This is the time to
contact, not the time care begins."
Boundary: no same-day care, arrival time or availability guarantee.

**CLM-008 Assessment before care (high) — 19 pages**
"Care is delivered only after a formal Medic Connect assessment. The assessment is proportionate
to the service requested and may take place immediately before a one-off or same-day
intervention rather than as a separate earlier appointment."
Boundary: does not imply a separate earlier appointment in every case.

**CLM-009 Credential collection and review (high) — 24 pages**
"Before a professional is deployed, Medic Connect collects and reviews identity documentation,
professional registration and qualification documents where the role requires them, references,
employment history, an interview and a competency assessment."
Boundary: document review, not regulator-side verification; not every role requires registration.

**CLM-010 Police and FCID checks (high) — 24 pages**
"Police and FCID checks are not carried out for every Medic Connect professional. In Lagos, a
check can be arranged on request."
Boundary: explicitly not universal.

**CLM-011 Role and assignment onboarding (medium) — 6 pages**
"Each professional completes onboarding for the role and the assignment before deployment,
covering safeguarding, confidentiality, infection prevention, escalation, documentation and the
competencies the assignment requires."

**CLM-012 Ambulance and medical transport (high) — 4 pages**
"Medic Connect arranges ambulance and medical transport through third-party providers. Medic
Connect does not operate its own ambulances."

**CLM-013 Medevac partnership (high, still draft) — 0 pages**
"Medic Connect coordinates medical transport and air ambulance (medevac) through third-party
partner providers. Partners are not named publicly."
Boundary: the previously registered partner name has been removed from the public wording; see
Issue 1.

**CLM-014 Pharmacy and laboratory (high) — 5 pages**
"Medic Connect arranges prescription supply, medication delivery, home sample collection and
laboratory testing through appropriately licensed pharmacies and laboratories."
Boundary: arranged through licensed third parties; Medic Connect does not operate them.

**CLM-015 Equipment (medium) — 2 pages**
"Medic Connect arranges care equipment for rental or purchase and sources further equipment from
suppliers."

**CLM-016 After-death coordination (high) — 1 page**
"Following a death at home, Medic Connect coordinates the appropriate third-party services for
the family. Medic Connect does not itself carry out mortuary, death verification or funeral
functions."

**CLM-017 Served markets (high) — 22 pages**
"Medic Connect currently serves Lagos, Abuja and the FCT, Ogun State, and Ibadan and Oyo State.
Requests elsewhere in Nigeria are considered subject to a serviceability review."
Boundary: no nationwide availability; Port Harcourt/Rivers is not included.

**CLM-018 24-hour coverage (high) — 4 pages**
"Twenty-four-hour care is continuous coverage provided by a rostered team working shifts with
handovers. It does not mean one professional remains on duty for 24 hours."

**CLM-019 No home birth (high) — 5 pages**
"Medic Connect does not provide home-birth services."

**CLM-020 Professional Omugwo (high) — 5 pages**
"Professional Omugwo is organised mother-and-baby support delivered by Medic Connect nurses and
caregivers. Depending on the package it combines maternal and newborn care, feeding support,
night support, meals and household help. It is provided by paid professionals, not by a family
member."
Boundary: broader than nursing; never presented as a relative.

**CLM-021 Diaspora coordination (medium) — 1 page**
"Medic Connect can act as the local care coordinator for a family living abroad, where the person
receiving care or their authorised representative has authorised the arrangement."

**CLM-022 Returning-to-Nigeria postpartum (medium) — 1 page**
"Medic Connect can coordinate a managed postpartum stay for a mother returning to Nigeria,
covering the care and the non-medical logistics agreed in the package."

**CLM-023 Staffing and managed workforce (medium) — 2 pages**
"Medic Connect supplies temporary, locum, contract and permanent healthcare and support staff,
fills hard-to-fill roles, builds teams, and runs managed workforce arrangements including
departments or wards where the contract provides for it."

**CLM-024 Operational facility management (medium) — 1 page**
"Medic Connect provides operational facility management where this is contracted.
Responsibilities in each engagement are those set out in that contract."

**CLM-025 Community and NGO programmes (medium) — 1 page**
"Medic Connect delivers community, NGO and public health programmes as a contracted delivery
organisation."

**CLM-026 Justice and correctional healthcare (high) — 1 page**
"Medic Connect supports healthcare in justice and correctional settings, subject to institutional
permission and contract."

**CLM-027 Clinical research support (high) — 1 page**
"Medic Connect provides operational support to clinical research and trials, including research
staffing, participant recruitment support, site and home visits, sample collection, study
coordination, follow-up and logistics. Medic Connect is not the study sponsor, ethics authority
or protocol owner unless contracted in that role."

**CLM-028 Incident recording and review (high) — 0 pages**
"Incidents during managed care are recorded, reviewed internally and followed up with the client."

**CLM-029 Complaints (medium) — 1 page**
"Clients can raise a complaint directly with Medic Connect. Complaints are investigated and
responded to."

**CLM-030 Supervision versus placement (medium) — 4 pages**
"Medic Connect supervises the professionals it deploys on managed assignments. Where a
professional is placed permanently with a client, day-to-day management passes to that client."

**CLM-031 Performance concerns (medium) — 2 pages**
"Where performance concerns arise, a professional may be removed from the assignment, retrained
or reviewed, and may be removed from Medic Connect work for serious or repeated misconduct."

**CLM-032 Not an emergency service (high) — 22 pages**
"Medic Connect is not an emergency service. In an emergency, contact the emergency services
rather than wait for a scheduled Medic Connect visit. Medic Connect staff recognise deterioration
within their competence, give immediate support within their scope, and coordinate escalation,
ambulance or hospital transfer."

**CLM-033 Information sharing (high) — 1 page**
"Care information is shared with the person receiving care and with the people they or their
authorised representative have authorised, subject to safeguarding and legal requirements. For
children, and for people who cannot give that authority themselves, a parent, guardian or
authorised representative provides it."

## B. The 16 canonical modules

The full proposed copy is held in the registry (`seo_modules.content.body`) and shown in the SEO
workspace. Summarised here with supporting claims, page usage and data dependencies.

**MOD-01 Trust and vetting — 25 pages.** Recruitment sequence (identity, registration and
qualifications where the role requires them, references, employment history, interview,
competency assessment), assignment onboarding, then two explicit limits: credentials are reviewed
rather than regulator-verified, and police/FCID checks are not universal but can be arranged in
Lagos. Closes with HEFAMAA, indemnity insurance and HFN membership.
Claims: CLM-009, CLM-010, CLM-011, CLM-001, CLM-002, CLM-003.

**MOD-02 Home assessment — 19 pages.** What the assessor reviews, what the assessment produces,
who conducts clinical and non-clinical assessments, proportionality for one-off and same-day
care, then the fixed fee and the refund condition.
Claims: CLM-008, CLM-005, CLM-006. Data dependency: fee `PUB-ASSESSMENT` rendered via the token
`{{fee:PUB-ASSESSMENT}}`; the amount is not typed into prose.

**MOD-03 Clinical scope — 12 pages.** Opens with the four limits (assessment, care plan,
professional scope, suitability of the home), then the service list, then the hospital boundary
and what happens when a need falls outside home care.
Claims: CLM-008, CLM-032 (referral and transfer route).

**MOD-04 Escalation and emergency boundary — 19 pages.** Not an emergency service, the escalation
route staff follow, third-party transport, and the safeguarding route.
Claims: CLM-032, CLM-012, CLM-013, CLM-033, CLM-028.

**MOD-05 Pricing logic — 21 pages.** Fixed assessment fee, fixed versus from prices, why ongoing,
live-in, overnight and package care is quoted after assessment, and disclosure of third-party
costs. No figure is written into the prose.
Claims: CLM-005, CLM-006, CLM-008. Data dependency: all public figures render from current public
`service_fees` records (34 registered: 20 fixed, 14 from).

**MOD-06 Coverage and serviceability — 24 pages.** The four served areas, how requests elsewhere
are handled, and why a start date follows assessment.
Claims: CLM-017, CLM-007.

**MOD-07 Care documentation and coordination — 11 pages.** What is recorded, care plan review
triggers, the named care management contact, and who records are shared with. States no client
portal.
Claims: CLM-028, CLM-033, CLM-008.

**MOD-08 Medication governance — 9 pages.** What nurses do within care plan and scope, the
explicit statement that Medic Connect administers but does not prescribe, and pharmacy and
laboratory arrangements.
Claims: CLM-014, CLM-009, CLM-008.

**MOD-09 Shift and handover model — 13 pages.** Definition of 24-hour coverage, roster
composition, handover, supervision, incident review and replacement.
Claims: CLM-018, CLM-030, CLM-028.

**MOD-10 Postnatal and Omugwo core — 5 pages.** Postnatal clinical care, professional Omugwo as
the broader package, and the antenatal and home-birth boundaries.
Claims: CLM-020, CLM-019.

**MOD-11 Child safeguarding and specialist childcare — 4 pages.** Childcare services, agreed
duties, specialist support routes, safeguarding onboarding and authorised pickup.
Claims: CLM-011, CLM-009. See Issue 2.

**MOD-12 B2B staffing and managed services — 3 pages.** Supply, team building, managed workforce
and facility management, and the statement that clinical governance sits where the contract
places it.
Claims: CLM-023, CLM-024, CLM-030.

**MOD-13 Talent opportunity and workforce model — 5 pages.** Candidate pool, application,
engagement types; matching factors; supervision versus placement; performance route. States
plainly that pool registration is not an offer of work.
Claims: CLM-030, CLM-031, CLM-009, CLM-023. JobPosting schema stays reserved for real vacancies.

**MOD-14 Diaspora and managed coordination — 1 page.** Coordinator role under authorisation, what
the package can cover, who receives updates, and the managed postpartum return.
Claims: CLM-021, CLM-022, CLM-033.

**MOD-15 Event medical cover — 1 page.** Scope, plan, deploy, on-site first response and triage,
escalation; plus the statement that Medic Connect is not an ambulance service.
Claims: CLM-032, CLM-012.

**MOD-16 Programme delivery and research support — 1 page.** Programme delivery, justice
healthcare, research operations, and the sponsor/ethics/protocol boundary.
Claims: CLM-025, CLM-026, CLM-027.

## C. Issues requiring a decision

1. **CLM-013 named medevac partner.** The registered claim names Flying Doctors Nigeria. The
   proposed wording keeps the capability and removes the name, and the claim stays in `draft`. A
   decision is needed: either supply the partner agreement evidence and approve the named version,
   or approve the generic version and retire the name.
2. **Authorised pickup for childcare (MOD-11).** The module states that only people named by the
   family are authorised for school pickup. This is a real operating rule in the intake process
   but has no governed claim. It needs either a new claim or removal from the module.
3. **Named care management contact (MOD-07).** Stated in the module, not covered by any claim.
   Same decision required.
4. **Safeguarding onboarding for child-facing roles (MOD-11).** CLM-011 covers onboarding
   generally; a child-specific statement would need its own claim if it is to be emphasised.
5. **CLM-004 (LAWMA) and CLM-028 (incident review) are attached to no page.** CLM-028 is used in
   module wording; the mapping should attach it to the managed-care pages. CLM-004 may be
   intentionally unused.
6. **Third-party cost disclosure (MOD-05).** The statement that third-party costs are set out
   before they are incurred is a commercial commitment with no governed claim behind it.
7. **Insurance scope (CLM-002).** Confirm whether public liability or employer cover also exists;
   the wording currently claims indemnity only.

No claim was created to close any of these. Nothing else is blocking.
